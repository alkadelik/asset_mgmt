Roles
Technician / Team lead
One or all members of the team have access to the app. This would ideally be the team lead however it has been observed that in some cases, only one team member carries out maintenance duties.
App goals for Technician/Team Lead
Track base stations to attend to daily
Inform technician of which assets within the base station to check - for routine maintenance
e.g. generator run hours, diesel level, janitorial checks, and others.
Inform technician of maintenance checklist on each asset
Let technician tick off maintenance items for each asset
Let technician enter extraordinary notes where necessary - during maintenance
Let technician enter extraordinary notes - after maintenance (until next maintenance period)
Inform technician of alarms
Inform technician of extraordinary maintenance task
Track request and receipt of consumables
Record usage of consumables per asset, per base station
Upload photos of assets and consumable usage - where necessary
Enter quantity of diesel supplied
Submit daily maintenance reports to cluster manager
Clock in and out (maintenance start, and end times)

Identify stations that are in bad and Fair conditions
View detail of maintenance report from each base station - including extraordinary notes
